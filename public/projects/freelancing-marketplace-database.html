What it does

A complete MySQL database for a Fiverr-style platform: users and roles, freelancers, clients, skills, categories, gigs, orders, payments and reviews.

How it works

  1. ModelA normalised relational schema, taken through BCNF.
  2. ProtectKeys, constraints and triggers keep reviews and ratings consistent.
  3. ReportStored procedures, indexes, an Order_Report view and analytical queries with joins, aggregation and subqueries.

What I did

  • Wrote the DDL and DML: tables, primary and foreign keys, constraints and seed data, plus many of the queries.
  • Worked hands-on on implementing and integrating the triggers for reviews and ratings.
