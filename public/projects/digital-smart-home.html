What it does

A Digital Logic Design prototype: lights switch on when someone walks in and off after there's no motion, and a fan turns on when the room gets warm.

How it works

  1. SenseA PIR HC-SR501 sensor detects motion.
  2. CombineA 74LS32 OR gate combines the sensor with a manual switch, so either can turn the light on.
  3. CoolA W1209 temperature controller switches the fan on above a set threshold.
