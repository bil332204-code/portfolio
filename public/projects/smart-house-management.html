What it does

Tracks how many electricity units a household uses, warns when usage gets close to the next tariff slab and points out which appliances use the most.

How it works

  1. RecordUsage data is saved to and read from files.
  2. WarnThe program compares totals with tariff thresholds.
  3. AdviseIt flags high-consuming equipment and gives basic recommendations.
